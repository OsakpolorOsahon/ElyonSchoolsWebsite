Purpose of this Policy

This handbook provides clear guidelines for staff conduct, responsibilities, and organizational structure. It was created with input from the entire school community to foster a positive, growth-oriented work environment for all staff—both teaching and non-teaching.

Staff Qualifications

Teaching Staff:
NCE with transcripts; 3–5 years’ experience is an added advantage.

National Teachers Institute (NTI) certificate.

National Diploma

Higher National Diploma

Teacher Registration Council of Nigeria Certification (TRCN)

A Bachelor's in Ed. Or a related course.

Support/Non-Teaching Staff: At least WAEC or relevant certification/experience (e.g., nurses, minders, administrators).

Job roles are detailed in each staff's employment letter.

General Expectations

All staff must:

Fulfill job descriptions and participate in evaluations.

Attend required meetings and training.

Offer timely feedback.

Teaching staff must maintain credentials, accurate records, and current curriculum and scheme of work

Work Assignments & Reassignments

Assignments are made by administrators and may change based on needs or performance. 


---

Disengagement

Staff intending to resign  must provide at least 30 days’ notice. Failure to do so may lead to forfeiture of the month's salary.

Supervision & Professional Development

Work Schedule

Full-time staff: 7:30 am – 3:00 pm (Mon–Thur), with a mandatory 30 minutes after closing hour. Additional meetings when required is compulsory.
